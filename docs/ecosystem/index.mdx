ZIO has a rich ecosystem of libraries and tools that enhance its capabilities and provide additional functionality, for web development, data processing, testing, and more.

Find a library by name or by purpose below. **Official** libraries are maintained by the ZIO team under the [ZIO organization](https://github.com/zio/), and include the blocks of [ZIO Blocks](https://zioblocks.com), which [zioblocks.com/reference](https://zioblocks.com/reference/) groups by the same categories. **Community** libraries are maintained by members of the ZIO community, and **compatible** libraries work with ZIO.
