=== Performance Analysis ===
This benchmark compares the performance of cats-effect race with ZIO race implementations.
The results show how ZIO race performs in different scenarios:
1. When the left side never completes (ZIO.never.race(ZIO.succeed(...)))
2. When the right side never completes (ZIO.succeed(...).race(ZIO.never))
3. The original implementation with both sides potentially completing

Note: The OptimizedRace implementation in OptimizedRace.scala aims to improve
performance by reusing the calling fiber for one side of the race, reducing
overhead by creating only one new fiber instead of two.
