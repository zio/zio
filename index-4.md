## Books

  
    The comprehensive book on ZIO, by John A. De Goes, Adam Fraser, and Milad Khajavi.
  
  
    Building complex applications with ZIO, by Bill Frasure, Bruce Eckel, and James Ward.
  

## Courses

  
    Free YouTube courses covering ZIO fundamentals, ZIO Streams, ZIO Kafka, and ZIO HTTP + Caliban.
  
  
    Paid courses covering ZIO fundamentals and the full-stack [ZIO Rite of Passage](https://rockthejvm.com/courses/zio-rite-of-passage).
  

## Stay in the loop

  
    The most active place for ZIO discussion, help, and links to new community content as it's published.

Don't wait for a docs PR — share it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/2ccFBr4) or [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/zio/zio/discussions) where the community will actually see it.
